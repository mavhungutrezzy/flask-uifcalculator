How to Check UIF Status Online

You can check your UIF claim online using the UIF's official online services. Have your South African ID number and the information used when you registered for UIF available before starting.

Step 1 — Open the UIF online service

Go to the official UIF online service used for individual UIF services at Ufiling Portal. Need help accessing your account? Read our uFiling Login Guide.

Step 2 — Sign in or register

If you already have an account, sign in using your registered details. If you have not registered, you may need to create an account and provide your personal information.

Step 3 — Find your UIF claim

After signing in, look for the section related to your UIF application, claim or benefit.

Step 4 — Check your claim status

Your claim may show a status explaining where it is in the process. The exact wording can differ depending on the UIF service and type of claim.

Step 5 — Check payment information

If your claim has been approved, check whether a payment has been processed or whether further action is required.

UIF Status Meanings Explained

One of the most confusing parts of checking UIF is understanding what the status means. A status does not always mean that you will receive money immediately. Your claim may still need to go through several steps.

This generally means that your application has been received by the UIF system. It does not necessarily mean that your claim has been approved. Your application may still need to be checked.

Your application is being reviewed. The UIF may need to check:
  • Your identity
  • Employment information & contributions
  • Reason for claiming
  • Employer information
  • Supporting documents
  • Banking details
Processing times can vary, especially when additional information is required.

An approved claim means the UIF has accepted the claim for the relevant benefit. However, approval does not always mean that the money has already reached your bank account. You may still need to wait for the payment process.

Payment processed normally means that a payment has been processed for your claim. The time before the money appears in your bank account depends on the payment process and your bank.

Payment made means a payment has been recorded as made. If the payment is not showing in your bank account after a reasonable period, check your banking information and UIF claim details.

A declined claim means the UIF has not approved the claim. If your claim has been declined, read our guide on why a UIF claim gets rejected or cancelled to understand the reasons and see how to correct missing information or request a review.

Why Is My UIF Status Still Pending?

A UIF claim can remain pending while the Department of Employment and Labour checks your application. There can be several reasons for a delay:

1. Application still being checked

UIF needs to verify the information submitted with your claim.

2. Employer information not confirmed

Some UIF processes depend on information submitted by your employer. If your employer has not submitted or corrected required information, your claim may take longer.

3. Documents are missing

Your application may require supporting documents. A missing document can prevent the claim from moving forward.

4. Banking information needs attention

Incorrect or outdated banking information can cause payment problems. Make sure your banking details are correct and belong to you.

5. Contributions need checking

UIF benefits are linked to UIF contributions. If contribution records are incomplete or incorrect, your claim may need further verification.

6. Additional information needed

Sometimes the department needs more information before making a decision.

Why Has My UIF Payment Not Arrived?

If your UIF claim has been approved but you have not received your money, first check your UIF status again. Also check:

  • Whether the payment has actually been processed
  • Whether your banking details are correct
  • Whether your bank account is active
  • Whether UIF requested additional information
  • Whether there is another outstanding step on your claim

If the UIF system shows that payment has been made but you cannot find the money, you should contact the Department of Employment and Labour or UIF for assistance.

Do not submit multiple applications for the same benefit simply because your payment is delayed.

How Long Does UIF Take to Pay?

There is no single guaranteed number of days that applies to every UIF claim. Processing depends on factors such as:

  • Type of UIF benefit
  • Whether the application is complete
  • Employer information
  • Contribution records
  • Supporting documents
  • Banking information
  • Whether additional verification is required

A straightforward application can move faster than one that needs corrections or additional information. If your claim has been waiting for a long time, checking the status and identifying any outstanding action is more useful than simply submitting another application.

What If My UIF Information Is Wrong?

If your UIF claim contains incorrect information, do not simply ignore it. Examples include:

  • Incorrect ID information
  • Wrong employer information
  • Incorrect banking details
  • Incorrect employment dates
  • Missing contributions
  • Incorrect contact information

The correct process depends on the type of information that needs to be changed. Contact UIF or the Department of Employment and Labour if you are unsure how to correct the information. Our UIF banking details and UI-2.8 guide may help if the issue is payment-related.

What If My Employer Did Not Pay UIF?

Your UIF contributions are normally linked to employment records and employer declarations. If you believe UIF contributions were deducted from your salary but your records do not show the expected information, you should investigate the issue. Keep copies of:

  • Payslips
  • Employment contract
  • Termination letter
  • UIF documents
  • Proof of deductions

These documents can help when you contact the relevant department about a contribution or employment-record problem. Read our missing UIF contributions guide for more detail.

Important UIF Scams to Avoid

Be careful when searching for UIF assistance online. Scammers may claim that they can:

  • Release your UIF payment
  • Speed up your claim
  • Fix your UIF status
  • Change your banking details
  • Get your payment immediately

You should never give another person your:

  • Banking password
  • ATM PIN
  • Online banking PIN
  • OTP
  • Card PIN

Be especially careful if someone contacts you unexpectedly and asks for money to release a UIF payment.

What UIF Actually Pays Out

It's a real fund with real numbers behind it. According to the UIF's own 2024/25 Annual Report, the Fund paid R18 billion in benefits to 3.1 million beneficiaries that year despite a slow economy, and closed the year with a R23 billion surplus.

That said, the process isn't always smooth. Reporting from Daily Maverick found that claimants commonly face delays beyond the standard processing window, long queues at Labour Centres, and recurring issues with the online filing platform — usually traced back to incomplete employer declarations or banking details that don't quite match. Knowing your estimated payout in advance, and having your documents ready before you apply, is the single biggest thing that keeps a claim moving.
