How the UIF Unemployment Payout Is Calculated

The Department of Employment and Labour uses a sliding scale formula based on your average salary and accumulated credit days.

Step 1 — Salary Remuneration Cap

UIF calculations cap gross monthly earnings at R17,712 per month (R212,544 annually). If you earn more than R17,712 per month, your benefit is calculated using R17,712 as the maximum baseline.

Step 2 — Daily Income Calculation

Daily Income = (Average Monthly Salary × 12) ÷ 365

Example for R15,000 monthly salary: R15,000 × 12 ÷ 365 = R493.15 per day.

Step 3 — Income Replacement Rate (IRR)

IRR = 29.2 + (7,173.92 ÷ (232.92 + Daily Income))

The IRR ranges between 38% for maximum earners and 60% for lower earners. This ensures lower-income workers receive a higher percentage of their salary.

Step 4 — Daily Benefit & Total Payout

Daily Benefit Amount = Daily Income × IRR
Total Estimated Benefit = Daily Benefit Amount × Credit Days

Your daily benefit is multiplied by your total earned credit days to determine your maximum payout.

Who Can Claim Unemployment UIF?

You qualify to claim UIF unemployment benefits if you were a contributing employee and became unemployed due to:

  • Retrenchment or operational requirements
  • Expiration of a fixed-term contract
  • Dismissal (unless due to gross misconduct)
  • Insolvency / Closure of employer company
Important: You cannot claim UIF unemployment benefits if you resigned voluntarily.

Documents Needed to File Your Claim

Before lodging your claim at a Labour Centre or via UIF Online, gather the following documents:

  • ID Document (Bar-coded ID book, Smart ID card, or passport)
  • Form UI-19 completed and signed by your former employer
  • Form UI-2.8 (Banking details confirmation stamped by your bank)
  • Notice of Termination or retrenchment letter from employer
  • Proof of Registration as a Work-Seeker
  • Recent payslips (last 6 months)
