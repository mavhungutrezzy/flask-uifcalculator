SASSA SRD R370 Grant 2026: Overview & Guide

Everything you need to know about the Social Relief of Distress R370 grant – from checking status and payment dates to applying and appealing declines.

SA Benefits & Calculators · Updated August 2026 · 9 min read

Quick overview: The SASSA SRD grant pays R370 per month to unemployed South African citizens and eligible residents aged 18 to 59 with monthly income under R624. You can check your R370 status online at srd.sassa.gov.za or via WhatsApp (082 046 8553).

What is the SRD R370 Grant?

The Social Relief of Distress (SRD) grant was established during the Covid-19 pandemic to provide temporary financial support to unemployed individuals with little to no monthly income. Originally set at R350 per month, the grant was increased to R370 per month in April 2024.

The national budget has extended the funding for the SRD R370 grant through to March 2027, ensuring continued support for millions of eligible beneficiaries across South Africa.

Key Facts About the R370 Grant

Grant Amount R370 per month per approved recipient.
Age Limit 18 to 59 years old.
Income Threshold Total monthly bank deposits must be less than R624 (Means Test).
Payment Schedule Paid monthly in batches, usually between the 23rd and the end of the month.
Reassessment Frequency Evaluated fresh every single month using automated system checks.

How to Check Your SRD R370 Status

You can track your monthly approval status through several easy channels:

  • Online Portal: Visit our main SASSA status check page or go directly to srd.sassa.gov.za.
  • WhatsApp: Send "SASSA" to 082 046 8553 and enter your ID number.
  • USSD Code: Dial *134*7737# from any phone (no data required).
  • Toll-Free Phone: Call 0800 60 10 11 and speak to an agent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do people still call it the R350 grant?

The grant launched in 2020 at R350 per month, so many people continue searching for "R350" out of habit. However, the official payout amount increased to R370 in April 2024.

Will the R370 grant increase again in 2026?

Any future increases to social grant amounts are announced during official national budget speeches by the Minister of Finance. As of now, R370 remains the active grant amount.

Can I get UIF and R370 at the same time?

No. Active receipt of UIF unemployment benefits disqualifies you from receiving the SRD R370 grant for those same months.
