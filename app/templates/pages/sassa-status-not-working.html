SASSA Status Check Not Working? How to Fix It & Alternatives

Is the SRD website failing to load, timing out, or throwing errors? Here is why the SASSA portal crashes, how to bypass connection issues, and backup ways to check your status right now.

SA Benefits & Calculators · Updated August 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer: If the official site is experiencing high traffic or errors, try checking your status on our status page, or use backup methods like WhatsApp or USSD.

Check SASSA Status

Why the SASSA Portal Stops Working

The official SRD status portal experiences frequent downtime and slowdowns due to several common technical factors:

  • Peak Traffic Spikes: Around payment dates (23rd to the end of the month), millions of grant holders try to log in at the same time, causing server timeouts.
  • Scheduled System Maintenance: SASSA updates its database records and runs monthly means test scripts outside normal hours or over weekends, during which status checks temporarily fail.
  • Browser Cache Issues: Stored cookies and cached data on your phone browser can prevent new status pages from rendering properly.
  • Network or Data Restrictions: Low phone data or unstable mobile signal can result in incomplete page loading or SSL handshake errors.

3 Fast Alternative Ways to Check Your Status

When the main portal is unresponsive, use these backup channels that operate on separate system infrastructure.

1. Official WhatsApp Bot

  1. Save the number 082 046 8553 in your phone contacts.
  2. Send a message saying SASSA or Status.
  3. Follow the prompts to enter your 13-digit ID number.
  4. The system returns your latest approval status and payment date directly in chat.

2. USSD Code (Zero Data Required)

Dial *134*7737# or *120*3210# on any mobile phone. Follow the menu prompts to input your South African ID number. Your status will display on your screen without requiring data.

3. Toll-Free Call Centre

Call 0800 60 10 11 from any phone. Select option 1 for SRD status queries and provide your ID number to an operator.

Troubleshooting Website Errors

If you want to access the web portal specifically, follow these browser troubleshooting steps:

  • Clear Cache & Cookies: Open your browser settings, clear browsing history and cached images, then reload srd.sassa.gov.za.
  • Try Incognito / Private Window: Open a private browsing tab to bypass old cached sessions.
  • Switch Wi-Fi / Mobile Data: If using Wi-Fi, turn it off and try mobile data (or vice versa) to rule out DNS routing glitches on your network.
  • Try Off-Peak Hours: Access the site early in the morning (before 07:00) or late at night (after 21:00) when traffic volume is lowest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does it say "System Error" when submitting my ID?

A "System Error" notice usually means SASSA's backend database is undergoing maintenance or experiencing heavy query load. Wait 30 minutes and try again or use WhatsApp.

Is there a difference between srd.sassa.gov.za and sassa.gov.za?

Yes. sassa.gov.za is the general department homepage, whereas srd.sassa.gov.za is the dedicated web portal specifically built for SRD R370 applications and status checking.

Does USSD cost airtime?

Standard USSD session fees (20c per 20 seconds) may apply depending on your network provider, but no data bundle is needed.
