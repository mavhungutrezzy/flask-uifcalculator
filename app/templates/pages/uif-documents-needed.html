UIF Documents Needed for Unemployment Claims

Prepare your UI-19, UI-2.8 banking form, ID or passport, payslips and work seeker proof before you submit a UIF unemployment claim.

UIF Calculators Team · Updated July 2026 · 7 min read

To claim UIF in South Africa, you usually need a completed UI-19 form, a UI-2.8 banking form, a copy of your 13-digit ID or passport, your last six payslips, and proof that you registered as a work seeker. You can apply at a Labour Centre or online through uFiling.

Core UIF Document Checklist

  • 13-digit bar-coded ID or passport: keep a certified copy of your South African ID, smart ID card or valid passport where applicable.
  • Form UI-19: completed by your employer to confirm employment status, employment history and the reason employment ended.
  • Form UI-2.8: banking details form for UIF payments. This may need to be stamped or confirmed by your bank.
  • Last six payslips: used to verify salary, employment and UIF contribution information.
  • Proof of work seeker registration: unemployment claimants must register as work seekers with the Department of Employment and Labour.
  • Service certificate: request this from your previous employer if UIF asks for extra employment confirmation.
  • Proof of residential address: not always required, but useful to keep ready if requested.

Where and When to Submit

Timeline
Submit your unemployment UIF claim within six months of becoming unemployed to avoid losing benefits.
Method
You can submit at your nearest Labour Centre or apply online through the official uFiling portal.

Why the UI-19 Form Matters

The UI-19 form helps UIF confirm that you are no longer working for your employer, when employment ended, and why it ended. Incorrect termination reasons can slow down or affect a claim.

Read the full UI-19 form guide if you need help understanding what the form does.

Reason for Termination

You generally cannot claim unemployment UIF if you resigned voluntarily. You may be able to claim if you were retrenched, dismissed subject to UIF rules, your fixed-term contract ended, or if constructive dismissal is proven through the CCMA.

For more detail, read can I claim UIF if I resigned?

Documents for Other Claim Types

The checklist above covers standard unemployment claims. Other benefit types need some of the same documents plus a few extras:

  • Maternity benefits: in addition to your ID and UI-2.8, you will need a medical certificate or birth certificate and employer confirmation of your maternity leave dates. See the maternity UIF documents guide for the full list.
  • Illness benefits: a medical certificate from a registered doctor covering the period you were unable to work, alongside your standard ID and banking documents.
  • Dependant’s benefits: a death certificate, proof of relationship to the deceased contributor, and the dependant’s own ID and banking details.

If you are not sure which category applies to you, the standard unemployment checklist is the safest starting point — UIF will request additional documents if your claim needs them.

After You Submit: Tracking Your Documents

Once your documents are in, UIF still needs to process and adjudicate your claim — this is usually where the waiting happens. Rather than calling the Labour Centre, you can track progress yourself online.

Our UIF status check online guide explains what each status label means, from “pending” through to “payment blocked,” and what to do at each stage. If your status has not moved for a while, that guide is the fastest way to work out whether the hold-up is a missing document, a banking issue, or just a normal processing delay.

Avoid Common Document Delays

Names & IDs
Your documents, bank account and UIF profile should match as closely as possible.
Declarations
Missing employer declarations can affect credit days and trigger adjudication delays — see our guide on missing UIF contributions if this applies to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a certified copy of my ID instead of the original?

Yes. A certified copy, no older than three months, is generally accepted both at a Labour Centre and when uploading documents through uFiling.

What if my employer refuses to sign my UI-19?

You can still open a claim without it, but UIF will usually pause your claim until the form is submitted. If your employer is uncooperative, the Labour Centre can advise on escalating the request directly.

Do I need all six payslips if I worked for less than six months?

No. Submit payslips covering however long you were employed, up to a maximum of six months. UIF will calculate your average earnings from whatever period you provide.

Can I submit documents online, or do I need to visit a Labour Centre?

Most people can complete the entire process through uFiling, including document uploads. A Labour Centre visit is only usually necessary if you run into a technical issue or your claim needs manual verification.
