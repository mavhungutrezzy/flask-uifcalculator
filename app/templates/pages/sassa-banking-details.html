SASSA Banking Details: How to Add or Update Your Bank Account

Wrong banking details are one of the top reasons SASSA payments fail. This guide shows you exactly how to add, change, or fix your bank account on the SRD portal.

SA Benefits & Calculators · Updated August 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: Update your banking details on the SRD portal to ensure your bank account matches your ID. You can track your approval and payment status on our status check page.

Check SASSA Status

Why Getting Banking Details Right Matters

If SASSA approves your SRD grant but your banking details are wrong, the payment fails. The money bounces back to SASSA, and you have to wait for the next payment cycle after fixing the problem. Common issues include:

  • Wrong account number – even one digit off will send the payment to the wrong place or cause it to bounce.
  • Closed or inactive account – if your account has been dormant or closed, the bank rejects the payment.
  • Name mismatch – the name on the bank account must match the name on your SASSA application and ID exactly.
  • Wrong account type – selecting "cheque" when you have a "savings" account (or vice versa) can cause payment failures.

How to Update Your Banking Details Online

  1. Go to srd.sassa.gov.za
  2. Look for "Change Banking Details" or "Update Bank Account"
  3. Enter your 13-digit ID number
  4. Verify with the OTP sent to your registered cellphone number
  5. Enter your new bank details:
    • Bank name (e.g., Capitec, FNB, Standard Bank, Absa, Nedbank)
    • Account type (savings or cheque/current)
    • Account number
  6. Confirm and submit

The change takes effect for the next payment cycle. If you have already been approved for the current month and the payment has been processed, it will use the old details for that month.

Requirements for Your Bank Account

  • Must be in your own name. SASSA will not pay into someone else's account. The account holder name must match your ID.
  • Must be an active account. Make sure the account is open and in good standing with your bank.
  • Must be a South African bank account. Accounts at major SA banks (Capitec, FNB, Standard Bank, Absa, Nedbank, TymeBank, African Bank, Postbank) are all accepted.
  • One account per applicant. You can only link one bank account to your SRD application at a time.

What If You Don't Have a Bank Account?

You can still receive your SRD grant without a bank account. In this case, SASSA may pay you through:

  • Cash send / eWallet. You receive an SMS with a voucher code that you can withdraw at any ATM without needing a card.
  • Post Office. Some payments can be collected at a Post Office branch with your ID document.

However, having a bank account is strongly recommended as it is the fastest and most reliable payment method. Most South African banks offer low-cost or free accounts. Opening one takes about 30 minutes at any branch with your ID document and proof of address.

Once you have a bank account, add the details to your SRD profile using the steps above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a family member's bank account?

No. SASSA requires the bank account to be in your own name, matching your ID details. Payments to third-party accounts are rejected.

How long before the new banking details take effect?

Changes are processed for the next payment cycle. If you update your details mid-month, the current month's payment may still use the old details.

Will the bank account deposits count in the means test?

Yes. SASSA screens all deposits in your linked bank account as part of the monthly means test. Any deposits totalling R624 or more may trigger a decline for that month.

I changed my banking details but my payment still failed. Why?

If you changed your details after the payment for that month was already processed, it would have used the old details. The new details apply from the next payment cycle.

Can I update banking details at a SASSA office?

Yes. If you cannot access the online portal, visit your nearest SASSA office with your ID document and new bank details (bank card or statement showing your account number).

Banking details sorted? Check your SASSA status to see if your application has been approved, or see SASSA payment dates to know when to expect your money.

Source: South African Social Security Agency. Banking requirements can change – always verify on srd.sassa.gov.za.
