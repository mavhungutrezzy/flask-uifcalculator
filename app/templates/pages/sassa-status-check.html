Alternative Ways to Check SASSA Status

You can verify your grant outcome across several official channels:

1

Online SASSA Portal

Use our instant tool above or visit the official SASSA portal at srd.sassa.gov.za.

2

WhatsApp Hotline (082 046 8553)

Save the number and send "SASSA" or "Status". Follow the prompts and enter your 13-digit ID number.

3

USSD Cellphone Code (*134*7371#)

Dial *134*7371# on any mobile phone (no data or airtime required). Follow the on-screen prompts.

4

Toll-Free SASSA Support (0800 60 10 11)

Call the official customer support line. Have your ID number ready for the consultant.

Understanding Your Status Outcomes

What each outcome means and what actions you need to take:

Your application meets all eligibility criteria for the given month. A pay date will be allocated once batch processing is completed for your payment method.

SASSA is currently cross-checking your details against databases (Home Affairs, SARS, UIF, NSFAS, and commercial banks). Verification runs automatically each month.

Your application did not pass the means test or income verification for that month. You have the right to lodge an appeal on srd.dsd.gov.za within 90 days of the decline notification.

SASSA needs you to provide or confirm your banking details on the official srd.sassa.gov.za portal. The bank account must be registered in your own name and ID number.
Watch Out for SASSA Scams

SASSA will never ask you to pay a fee to check status, speed up your application, or release payment. Don't send your ID, PIN, or banking details to unknown WhatsApp numbers or unverified websites. Always use the official srd.sassa.gov.za website or verified SASSA channels.
