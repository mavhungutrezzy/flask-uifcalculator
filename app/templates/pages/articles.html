1. Calculating Your UIF Payout

Before submitting your claim, it helps to understand what you are entitled to receive. UIF payouts depend on your salary history, the duration of your contributions, and the sliding scale rate set by legislation.

Quick tip: Use our UIF Unemployment Calculator to estimate your monthly payout, credit days, and total benefit in under a minute.

The UIF formula applies a salary ceiling and evaluates your average monthly earnings over the last four years. Credit days accumulate as you work, providing roughly one day of benefits for every four days worked. If you want the full breakdown, read our guide on how to calculate UIF payout or learn what your results mean in what to do next.

2. How to Claim UIF Online

The uFiling portal is where most UIF claims begin. You will need to create an account, select your benefit category, upload supporting documentation, and verify your banking details. While the steps are straightforward, small details can cause unnecessary delays.

Our step-by-step guide covers how to claim UIF online, guiding you from registration through to submitting your initial payment request on the portal.

We also explain what actions to take if the portal requests additional documentation, indicates missing employer declarations, or rejects an application.

3. Documents You Need

Documentation is where many applications experience delays. The Department of Employment and Labour requires specific forms and verified information before processing payments. Key documents include:

  • Your valid South African ID document
  • The UI-19 form completed by your employer
  • Verified banking details using the UI-2.8 form
  • Proof of active work-seeking efforts

Review the complete checklist in our UIF documents needed guide. If you are applying for maternity UIF, requirements differ slightly, and we detail those in our maternity UIF documents guide.

4. Checking Your Status (UIF & SASSA)

After submitting your application, monitoring your claim status ensures you can respond quickly to any queries. Status updates such as “pending,” “approved,” or “payment blocked” indicate different stages of review.

Our comprehensive guide on UIF status check online explains what each status code means and how to track progress across online portals, WhatsApp, and USSD channels.

If you are also checking a social grant application, read our SASSA status check guide or use our instant SASSA status check tool to verify your SRD R370 outcome.

If online channels do not resolve your query, consult the UIF call centre contact list for official support numbers before visiting a local office.

5. Payments, Delays & Credit Days

Benefit payments do not always follow a fixed timetable. Processing times can vary due to missing employer declarations, unverified bank details, or processing backlogs.

UIF payment dates and delays outlines why payments might be delayed and what timeframes to expect.

Credit days determine the total period you are eligible to receive benefit payouts, up to a maximum of 365 days based on your work history. Read our UIF credit days guide to see how credit days accrue. If your employer failed to declare your monthly contributions, review our guide on missing UIF contributions to resolve non-compliance issues.

6. Common Problems & Rejections

A declined or delayed claim can be stressful, but many issues are resolveable. Declines usually stem from incomplete paperwork, unverified bank details, or missing service records.

Our guide on why UIF claims get rejected walks through common refusal reasons and step-by-step solutions. If your claim was cancelled rather than rejected, our article explains the key differences and remedies.

7. Special Cases: Maternity, Contracts & Resignation

Maternity UIF

Maternity benefits follow specific guidelines and documentation requirements. If you are planning maternity leave, review our overview of maternity leave in South Africa to understand leave duration, employer notice, and how UIF complements your income.

When submitting your claim, ensure you have medical certificates, employer attestations, and verified banking forms. Our maternity UIF documents guide provides the complete document checklist.

Fixed-term contracts

When a fixed-term contract expires, you may qualify for unemployment benefits provided your employer declared your employment history. Learn what to check on your forms in our guide on UIF for fixed-term contracts.

Resignation

Voluntary resignation generally disqualifies applicants from standard unemployment benefits, though specific exceptions exist. If resignation occurred under involuntary circumstances such as constructive dismissal, you may still be eligible. Read can I claim UIF if I resigned to evaluate your options.
