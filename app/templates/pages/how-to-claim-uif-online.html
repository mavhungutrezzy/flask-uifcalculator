How to Claim UIF Online in South Africa

Apply for UIF benefits online, upload or confirm your information, and track your claim after submission.

UIF Calculators Team · Updated July 2026 · 6 min read

Employees now apply for UIF benefits through the UIF Online portal. It supports online applications for unemployment, maternity, illness and adoption benefits. The exact screens can change, but the core flow is usually the same: register or log in, select your benefit, confirm your information, and submit.

You may still see the older system referred to as uFiling. Employee benefit claims have moved to the newer UIF Online portal, while uFiling itself now mainly handles employer declarations. If you were sent an old uFiling benefit link, look for UIF Online instead, since that's the current route for individual claims.

Before You Start

Have these ready before you open the portal, to avoid stopping halfway through.

  • Your South African ID number or valid identity document
  • Your UIF Online login details, or time to register a new profile
  • Your employer and termination details, including the reason you stopped working
  • Banking details and UIF documents such as the UI-19 and UI-2.8, where required

Step by Step Online Claim Process

1. Open the portal

Go to the official UIF Online login page. Avoid third-party sites that ask for your ID or banking details outside this official domain.

2. Log in or register

Create a profile if you have not used the portal before. You'll need your ID number to register, and you may need to confirm your identity through an SMS or email step.

3. Choose the benefit type

Select the relevant UIF benefit, such as unemployment or maternity. If you're not sure which one applies to your situation, our UIF unemployment calculator and maternity and leave calculator can help you check before you commit to a benefit type.

4. Confirm your information

Check your personal details, employment information and banking details carefully. A small mismatch here, such as a surname that doesn't match your bank account, is one of the most common reasons a payment stalls later. See the UIF banking details and UI-2.8 guide if anything looks off.

5. Submit the application

Keep your reference number somewhere safe and check your status regularly rather than waiting to be contacted.

If your banking details are incorrect on the system, official guidance says you may need to update them using the UI-2.8 form before a payment can be released.

After You Apply

Use the claim status screen to track progress. Status messages such as Sent to Assessor, Payment Reserved or Sent to Paymaster show where your claim currently sits in the process. None of these mean the money has landed in your account yet, only that your claim is moving through a particular stage.

For help understanding what each status actually means, read the UIF status check guide. If a status hasn't changed in weeks, our payment dates and delays guide covers the most common reasons for that and what to do about it.

Estimate Your Benefit First

Before or after applying, you can use the UIF unemployment calculator to estimate your benefit amount, credit days and possible payout duration. Knowing roughly what to expect makes it easier to spot if something looks wrong once your claim is processed.

Once you've applied, it also helps to know what typically happens next. Our after you calculate your UIF guide walks through realistic processing timelines and what to do if your claim doesn't move as expected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UIF Online the same as uFiling?

They're related but not identical. Employee benefit claims have moved to UIF Online, while uFiling is now mainly used for employer declarations and contributions. If you're an individual applying for a benefit, UIF Online is the correct starting point.

What if I don't have a UIF reference number yet?

You don't need one to register. New users can create a profile directly on the portal, and a reference number is generated once you submit your claim.

Can I apply online if my claim involves a dispute, like a disputed dismissal?

You can still start online, but complicated cases such as a disputed dismissal or missing employer records are often easier to resolve in person, where a claims officer at a labour centre can look at your specific situation directly.

What happens if I submit the wrong benefit type by mistake?

Contact the UIF call centre or your nearest labour centre as soon as you notice, since correcting this after submission usually needs to go through a person rather than the portal itself.

Do I need to reapply every month?

No, but ongoing unemployment claims usually require a continuation step roughly every few weeks to confirm you're still unemployed and still eligible. Missing this step can pause your payments even if you still qualify.
